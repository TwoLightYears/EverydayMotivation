# Cleared For Stoop

**Pairing:** Peregrine Falcon (*Falco peregrinus*) as Fighter Pilot

## The hook
In an October 1999 skydiver-assisted telemetry drop with falconer Ken Franklin, the trained peregrine "Frightful" was clocked at **389 km/h (242 mph)** in a full stoop — the fastest self-powered dive ever measured on an animal. She keeps visual lock through that plunge because a translucent third eyelid, the **nictitating membrane**, sweeps sideways across each eye several times a second, wiping airborne particles while she still sees the target.

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 x 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette (drawn from live peregrine coloration):**
  - `#0B1420` — deep slate navy (adult peregrine back, deepened into the void)
  - `#3E4A5A` — mid slate blue-gray (silhouette midtone, HUD strokes)
  - `#8E9AA8` — pale slate (fine HUD ticks, grid, secondary type)
  - `#EDE0C6` — breast cream (title / focal type / falcon highlights)
  - `#F0B420` — cere & eye-ring yellow (accents, warning ticks, callout)
- **Mood:** cold, precise, cinematic-aerodynamic
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A tall central HUD/gunsight window (drafted with corner brackets) framing a full-stoop peregrine silhouette rendered in cream against slate navy — wings tucked, tail closed, plunging.
  - A vertical **airspeed tape** on the right (climbing to 389 KM/H) and a vertical **altitude tape** on the left (winding down); a fine graticule graticule overlays the frame.
  - A small circular inset — an aviator gauge — showing the falcon's eye with the nictitating membrane sweeping across it laterally.
  - Strong lower type lockup: uppercase ROLE tag, large display title, single-line hook.
- **Motion:** One primary spring-eased motion — the peregrine accelerates from the top of the HUD frame toward the bottom with an ease-in cubic (dramatizing the stoop), while the airspeed tape climbs and the altitude tape descends. The eye inset ticks a nictitating-membrane wipe on a ~1 Hz cadence. Roughly a 5-second loop.
