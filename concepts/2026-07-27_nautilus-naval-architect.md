# The Ambulatory Pressure Vessel

**Pairing:** Chambered Nautilus (*Nautilus pompilius*) as Naval Architect

## The hook
The nautilus grows its shell as a strict logarithmic spiral of gas-filled chambers linked by a single living tube — the **siphuncle** — that osmotically pumps liquid out so gas can diffuse in, holding the sealed chambers at roughly **1 atm** even while the animal hovers at ~400 m below the surface, where the surrounding sea presses in at more than **40 atm** (Denton & Gilpin-Brown, *J. Mar. Biol. Assoc. UK*, 1966).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette** — dark blueprint drawn from the nautilus's real coloration:
  - `#08192B` — deep abyssal navy (paper / substrate)
  - `#12304F` — mid-blueprint navy (grid / secondary panels)
  - `#F5EBD8` — nacreous ivory (primary ink)
  - `#E9D9A2` — pearl highlight (chamber wash, minor accents)
  - `#B54B2C` — rust-red tiger stripe (the nautilus's own aposematic banding; used sparingly for the callout and pressure indicator)
- **Mood:** exacting, hushed, subaqueous
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A large cross-section of the shell along its coiling plane, rendered as an ivory line drawing on navy blueprint paper — chambers numbered outward from the protoconch, the siphuncle a fine continuous conduit threading every septum.
  - A vertical **depth / pressure scale** hugging the left margin (0 m / 1 atm → 400 m / 41 atm), with a slow-moving depth marker.
  - A rust-red callout leader tagged **SIPHUNCLE — OSMOTIC BALLAST** pointing to the central tube.
  - A drafting frame with corner tick marks, faint grid, and a caption strip: *FIG. 1 · CHAMBERED PRESSURE VESSEL · NAUTILUS POMPILIUS*.
  - Type block below: uppercase ROLE tag ("NAVAL ARCHITECT"), display headline in italic Playfair ("Built for the abyss."), one-line hook in Inter.
- **Motion:** primary motion = the spiral **builds outward** chamber by chamber on a spring, each new chamber briefly washing to pearl as it fills with gas, then settling to the ivory line weight. Concurrently the depth marker eases down the vertical scale from 0 to ~400 m and a small pressure-gauge needle on the outermost chamber sweeps clockwise. Calm, ~5 s loop.
