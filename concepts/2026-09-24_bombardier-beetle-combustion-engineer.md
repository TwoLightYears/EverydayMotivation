# The Beetle That Built a Pulsejet

**Pairing:** Bombardier Beetle (*Brachinus* spp.) as Combustion Engineer

## The hook
Inside a Kevlar-hard reaction chamber in its abdomen, a bombardier beetle mixes hydroquinones and 25% hydrogen peroxide with catalase and peroxidases, ejecting a 100 °C benzoquinone spray in a rapid pulsed jet at ~500 discrete pulses per second — a biological pulsejet whose intermittent combustion cycle is functionally identical to the V-1 flying bomb's engine (Dean, Aneshansley, Edgerton & Eisner, *Science* 248 (1990): 1219–1221).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette:** (drawn from *Brachinus crepitans*'s real coloration)
  - `#0B0E14` — near-black elytra ground (deep base)
  - `#D74915` — vermilion pronotum / legs (the beetle's signature warning color)
  - `#F2A63A` — reaction heat amber (spray plume, glow)
  - `#F0E7D2` — vellum cream (paper of a patent drawing)
  - `#4E5866` — draftsman graphite (annotation, thin rules)
- **Mood:** exacting, technical, quietly menacing — an engineering brief written in blood-rust and vellum
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A profile schematic of the beetle, elytra rendered as a cutaway to reveal the two-chambered abdominal apparatus: **reservoir** (hydroquinones + H₂O₂), **reaction chamber** (catalase, peroxidases), **exit valve**, and a pulsed vermilion-amber spray plume exiting the abdomen tip.
  - A patent-drawing frame with a title cartouche, revision marks, and a dashed callout system linking parts to numbered legends.
  - A pulse-frequency oscillogram in the lower band — an evenly spaced burst pattern annotated "500 Hz".
  - Type block in the lower-left: display title in italic serif, uppercase ROLE tag with generous tracking, single-line hook.
- **Motion:** the spray plume pulses in tight, staccato bursts (drawn at the real ~500 Hz frequency, damped visually to a legible 6 Hz for the eye), while the oscillogram sweeps left-to-right on a spring, and the callout leaders draw in sequence. A 5-second loop.
