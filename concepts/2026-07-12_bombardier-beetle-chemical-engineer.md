# The Pocket Reactor

**Pairing:** Bombardier Beetle as Chemical Engineer

## The hook
The bombardier beetle (subfamily Brachininae) stores hydroquinone with hydrogen peroxide in one reservoir and the enzymes catalase + peroxidase in another; when threatened it mixes them in a reaction chamber and fires a boiling **100 °C** spray from a swivelling abdominal nozzle in **~500 discrete pulses per second** — a working hypergolic micro-reactor described in Aneshansley & Eisner, *Science* 165 (1969) 61–63, and stroboscopically resolved by Dean, Aneshansley, Edgerton & Eisner, *Science* 248 (1990) 1219–1221.
