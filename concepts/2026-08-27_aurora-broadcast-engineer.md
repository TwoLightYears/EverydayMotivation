# The Station on 557.7 nm

**Pairing:** Aurora Borealis as Broadcast Engineer

## The hook
The aurora's signature green is a *forbidden* transition of atomic oxygen — O(¹S→¹D) at exactly **557.7 nm**, with a metastable radiative lifetime of ~0.7 seconds (Einstein A ≈ 1.26 s⁻¹). That lifetime is slow enough that below ~100 km, collisions quench the excited state before it can emit; the green only survives in the thin thermosphere above. Every green aurora on Earth transmits on the same quantum-locked channel.
