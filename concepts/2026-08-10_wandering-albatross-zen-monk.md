# The Zen Monk

**Pairing:** Wandering Albatross (*Diomedea exulans*) as Zen Monk

## The hook
A sheet of tendon in the shoulder mechanically locks the wandering albatross's 3.5-metre wings in the extended position, so the bird can glide ~1,000 km a day and circumnavigate the Southern Ocean in about 46 days with a heart rate scarcely above resting — flight as effortless as sitting still (Pennycuick 1982; Weimerskirch et al., *Nature* 2000; Croxall et al., *Science* 307, 2005).

- **Mood:** meditative, vast, effortless

  - A fine anatomical leader line from the bird's shoulder joint labelled "SHOULDER-LOCK TENDON — MECHANICAL"

- **Motion:** one primary spring-eased motion — the bird slides in from the frame edge and settles into a slow dynamic-soaring oscillation (subtle roll of ±4°, subtle vertical bob) while the dashed circumnavigation track draws itself around the pole. A 6-second loop.
