# The Choreographer of the Sun

**Pairing:** Honeybee (*Apis mellifera*) as Choreographer

## The hook
A returning forager honeybee performs a figure-eight *waggle dance* on the vertical face of the comb whose central waggle-run encodes navigation as choreographic notation: the run's angle relative to gravity's vertical maps one-to-one onto the flight bearing relative to the sun's azimuth, and each additional second of waggling adds roughly 1 km of distance to the food source — a symbolic language decoded by Karl von Frisch (Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, 1973).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 x 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette:**
  - `#0B0C10` — hive dark (substrate / negative space)
  - `#F2B705` — bee yellow (the dancer & the waggle-run)
  - `#F58A0C` — amber comb (secondary warm, arcs)
  - `#F5E8C7` — pale honey / wax highlight (type accents)
  - `#8A8F99` — cool draftsman gray (notation, secondary type)
- **Mood:** exacting, warm-technical, choreographic-meets-cartographic
- **Key visual elements:**
  - The choreographic figure-eight itself, drawn as a chalked "notation": two open arcs meeting at a bold, dashed waggle-run tilted precisely 35° off vertical.
  - A gravity arrow (↓) locking the vertical axis; a small stylised sun icon in the upper-right locking the celestial axis. Both angles drawn identically to make the mapping self-evident.
  - A perpendicular tick-scale beside the waggle-run reading "1 s ≈ 1 KM", with rhythmic waggle tick-marks along the run itself.
  - Strong type block lower-left: uppercase ROLE tag, large display title, single-line hook.
- **Motion:** the figure-eight traces itself onto the hive — right arc, waggle-run down the tilted axis (with waggle tick-marks pulsing along it), left arc, back to origin — driven by a single spring easing. Once traced, a soft rhythmic pulse walks the waggle-run to dramatize the 1 s ≈ 1 km encoding. A 5-second loop.
