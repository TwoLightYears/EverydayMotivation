# The Million-Degree Glassmaker

**Pairing:** Lightning as Glassblower

## The hook
A cloud-to-ground lightning channel reaches peak temperatures near **30,000 K** — roughly five times the surface of the sun — and can fuse sandy soil along its path in under a millisecond into hollow, branching glass tubes called **fulgurites**: petrified casts of the strike's exact geometry, made almost entirely of shocked SiO₂ (lechatelierite).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 x 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette:**
  - `#0A0F1F` — storm indigo (sky / substrate)
  - `#C9E1FF` — cold plasma blue (bolt core outline, sky glow)
  - `#F7F3E1` — filament white (bolt center-line, key type)
  - `#E88A2E` — molten silica amber (fulgurite tube, hot glass)
  - `#3A2A16` — dark sand shadow (subsurface earth)
- **Mood:** incandescent, hushed, sublime — the split-second after the flash.
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A **single vertical composition**: night sky above, cross-section of sandy earth below, a hard **ground line** as a horizon.
  - The **lightning bolt** as a jagged plasma trace — hair-thin filament white core inside a wider soft blue halo — striking down through the sky.
  - Below the horizon, a **fulgurite** in molten amber that echoes the exact geometry of the bolt above: same branching, but embedded in the earth as glass — the "workpiece" the glassblower left behind.
  - Draftsman-style callouts: temperature (`≈ 30,000 K`), duration (`< 1 ms`), material (`SiO₂ → lechatelierite`), depth scale.
  - The type block below the image: uppercase ROLE tag, large Playfair italic title, one clean sans hook line.
- **Motion:** A single primary spring-easing motion — the bolt strikes top-down over ~10 frames; the fulgurite ignites and draws downward from the strike point mirroring the bolt's branches; a soft after-glow decays. Loops as a calm 5-second hero cycle.
