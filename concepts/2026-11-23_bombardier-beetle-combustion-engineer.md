# The Pulse-Jet Chemist

**Pairing:** Bombardier Beetle (*Brachinus* spp.) as Combustion Engineer

## The hook
A bombardier beetle stores hydroquinones and hydrogen peroxide in separate reservoirs and injects them into a reinforced reaction chamber lined with **catalase and peroxidase**; the exothermic reaction flashes the mixture to **~100 °C** and ejects it as a **~500 Hz pulsed spray** — a self-aimed, oscillating micro-combustor whose valve dynamics were later studied as an analog for pulsed-combustion jet engines (Aneshansley, Eisner, Widom & Widom, *PNAS* 96, 1999: 9705–9709).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette:** (drawn from *Brachinus*' real elytra / pronotum coloration)
  - `#0B0C10` — carapace black (substrate / negative space)
  - `#E85A2B` — orange pronotum (the warm trigger / head)
  - `#F4A869` — ember glow (spray highlight / caution)
  - `#2E4A66` — steely elytra blue (schematic secondary / rules)
  - `#A8A196` — chamber beige (schematic strokes / type secondary)
- **Mood:** precise, mechanical, volatile-under-control
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A patent-drawing cross-section of the beetle's abdomen: two labeled reservoirs (hydroquinone / H₂O₂) feeding into a reaction chamber marked with catalyst, firing out a nozzle at the tip.
  - The beetle's silhouette in profile sits above/behind the schematic, aligned so its abdomen *is* the engine.
  - A pulsed staccato spray arc leaving the nozzle — individual bursts, not a continuous stream.
  - Strong lower type lockup: display-serif title, uppercase ROLE tag, single-line hook.
- **Motion:** the reaction chamber "primes" on a spring (fill animation), then the spray fires as five discrete pulses on an ease-out — mirroring the real ~500 Hz pulse train. A gentle heat shimmer rides the barrel between bursts. 5-second loop.
