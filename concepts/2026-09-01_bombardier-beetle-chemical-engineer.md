# The Beetle That Owns a Reactor

**Pairing:** Bombardier Beetle (*Brachinus*) as Chemical Engineer

## The hook
Each side of a *Brachinus* beetle's abdomen carries a reservoir of ~25 % hydroquinone and ~10 % hydrogen peroxide that is valved into a thick-walled reaction chamber lined with catalases and peroxidases; the exothermic reaction generates 1,4-benzoquinone at ~100 °C and expels it as a pulsed jet at roughly 500 pulses per second (Aneshansley & Eisner, *Science* 165 (1969) 61–63; Dean, Aneshansley, Edgerton & Eisner, *Science* 248 (1990) 1219–1221).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette:**
  - `#0F1116` — deep carbon substrate (the drafting field)
  - `#E4571B` — Brachinus pronotum orange (the beetle's real red-orange head/thorax)
  - `#F5C542` — reactive-yellow spray warning (jet + heat gauge)
  - `#8FE0D8` — cool reactant fluid (pale cyan draftsman ink for H₂O₂)
  - `#C7CBD3` — engineering-blueprint gray (leader lines, secondary type)
- **Mood:** precise, industrial, quietly dangerous
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A technical cross-section of the beetle's rear abdomen in strict side profile — the two reservoirs (labelled H₂O₂, C₆H₄(OH)₂), the valve, the thick-walled reaction chamber with a temperature gauge climbing to 100 °C, and the aimed nozzle.
  - The signature engineer's prop is the isometric jet firing out of the nozzle, pulsing rhythmically toward the frame edge — the beetle's chemical weapon rendered like an industrial process schematic.
  - Strong type block in the lower-left: uppercase ROLE tag, italic display title, one-line hook. Engineering annotations (leader lines, callout numbers) tie diagram to caption.
- **Motion:** the two reactant fluids charge from reservoirs through the valve into the reaction chamber (spring easing, ~1.5 s); the chamber then flashes hot and the nozzle fires a pulsed benzoquinone jet at ~5 visible pulses per second (a compressed stand-in for the real 500 Hz), holding a calm loop for 5 s.
