# The Puppeteer Without a Brain to Read

**Pairing:** Ophiocordyceps unilateralis (Zombie-Ant Fungus) as Puppeteer

## The hook
When *Ophiocordyceps unilateralis* infects a *Camponotus* carpenter ant, 3D-reconstruction electron microscopy shows the fungus weaving a physical network of interconnected cells through — and around — the ant's mandibular muscle fibres, occupying up to ~40% of the biomass inside the head, while notably leaving the brain untouched; it then drives the ant to clamp its jaws onto the underside of a leaf at ~25 cm above the forest floor before killing it (Fredericksen et al., *PNAS* 114, 12590–12595, 2017).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 x 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette:**
  - `#0B0F0C` — understory ink (background)
  - `#1E2A20` — deep forest green (leaf mass, panels)
  - `#C9884A` — Ophiocordyceps amber (fungal stroma, accent lines)
  - `#F0D48A` — pale spore-glow highlight
  - `#8A8478` — botanical-plate gray (type secondary, grid)
- **Mood:** eerie, exacting, biological-macabre
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A vintage botanical/entomological specimen plate: a horizontal leaf near the upper third with the carpenter ant clamped upside-down under its midvein, jaws latched.
  - A single fungal stroma erupts through the ant's head and curves upward past the leaf, ending in a bulbous perithecial club with tiny drifting ascospores.
  - Overlaid: a hair-thin marionette control-cross floats above the leaf; threads descend into the ant's mandibles, thorax, and legs — the fungus rendered as a puppeteer.
  - Callouts: a `~25 cm` height rule with tick marks along the plate's left edge; an inset cross-section of the mandibular muscle showing the fungal network (Fredericksen et al.).
  - Type block at the lower third: uppercase ROLE tag, large italic display title, single-line hook. Strict left-align to a margin grid; no centered layout.
- **Motion:** the fungal stroma slowly extends upward from the ant's head on a spring easing, its perithecial tip swelling; a single, minute mandible-clamp snaps into the vein early; the marionette strings hold taut, breathing almost imperceptibly. A 5-second loop.
