# The Beetle That Ships a Pulse-Combustion Reactor

**Pairing:** Bombardier Beetle (*Brachinus* spp.) as Chemical Engineer

## The hook
In the beetle's abdomen, an inert reservoir of hydroquinones and 25% hydrogen peroxide is shot through a muscular valve into a hard-walled reaction chamber lined with catalase and peroxidase; the enzymes flash the mix to ~100 °C benzoquinone spray that exits a rotatable turret in ~500 discrete pulses per second — a working micro pulse-combustion reactor documented by high-speed synchrotron X-ray at Argonne (Arndt et al., *Science* 348:6234, 2015; Eisner & Aneshansley, *PNAS* 96:9705, 1999).
