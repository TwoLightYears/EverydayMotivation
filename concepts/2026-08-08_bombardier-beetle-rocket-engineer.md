# The Beetle That Built a Pulsejet

**Pairing:** Bombardier Beetle (*Brachinus* spp.) as Rocket Engineer

## The hook
When threatened, the bombardier beetle discharges a boiling defensive jet by injecting a hydroquinone / hydrogen-peroxide reservoir into a hardened reaction chamber lined with catalase and peroxidase; the exothermic reaction reaches ~100 °C and is expelled through a rotatable nozzle as a train of ~500 discrete micro-explosions per second — a passive pressure-relief oscillator that anticipated the pulsejet by ~200 million years (Aneshansley & Eisner, *Science* 165 (1969) 61–63; Dean et al., *Science* 248 (1990) 1219–1221; Beheshti & McIntosh, *Bioinspir. Biomim.* 2 (2007) 57–64).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait poster).
- **Palette (drawn from the beetle's real coloration and its spray):**
  - `#0F0B08` — deep beetle-elytra black (background / ink).
  - `#151714` — mid engine-panel green-black (drafting substrate).
  - `#D46A24` — bombardier-thorax orange (structural lines, key accent).
  - `#F6C56A` — ochre-buff (highlights, ticks, hero copy).
  - `#FF4B1F` — alarm spray red-orange (the jet itself).
  - `#8A8F99` — draftsman cool gray (grid, secondary type).
- **Mood:** patent-drawing precision, controlled violence, engineering awe.
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A cutaway "engine schematic" of the beetle's abdomen — reservoir → valve → reaction chamber → nozzle — rendered as a technical blueprint with call-out lines and part labels (RESERVOIR · CATALASE / PEROXIDASE · REACTION CHAMBER · NOZZLE).
  - The nozzle firing a stroboscopic burst of discrete droplets — one droplet per pulse — flying to the right, each drop slightly rotated and offset to imply the ~500 Hz cadence.
  - A subordinate oscilloscope-style trace along the bottom edge of the drafting frame plotting pressure vs. time at ~500 Hz.
  - Strong type block in the lower-left: uppercase ROLE tag, italic display title, and a single-line hook.
- **Motion:** the schematic assembles line-by-line (thin white draft strokes → color-in), then the reaction chamber flashes and the pulse train fires rightward with spring easing; the oscilloscope trace sweeps across in sync. A ~5 s loop.
