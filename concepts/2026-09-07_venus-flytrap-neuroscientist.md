# The Plant That Counts

**Pairing:** Venus Flytrap (*Dionaea muscipula*) as Neuroscientist

## The hook
The Venus flytrap has no neurons, yet it *counts*: mechanical touches on the trigger hairs inside each lobe generate stereotyped action potentials, and the plant integrates them — two spikes within about 20 seconds slam the trap shut, and five or more open jasmonate-driven digestive glands and sodium channels (Böhm et al., *Current Biology* 26 (2016) 286–295; Suda et al., *Nature Plants* 6 (2020) 1219–1224).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 x 1350 (portrait poster).
- **Palette (5 hex values, drawn from the plant's real coloration; tints/shades allowed):**
  - `#0F1410` — deep bog-substrate ink (background / negative space)
  - `#7EA83B` — outer lobe leaf-green (trap exterior)
  - `#7B1F1F` — crimson trap-interior (the anthocyanin gland field)
  - `#EDE7C3` — trigger-hair / paper-annotation cream
  - `#C6D96A` — chartreuse "phosphor" (oscilloscope trace + spikes)
- **Mood:** clinical-organic, exact, uncanny.
- **Key visual elements:**
  - **Focal object:** a top-down stylised trap — two lobes flanking a central midrib, crimson interior, six trigger hairs standing on the flat surface, cilia teeth fanned along the outer curves.
  - **Role's signature prop:** a neuroscientist's *oscilloscope trace* — a phosphor-green baseline running horizontally across the top of the panel, spike-marker pulses appearing one after another; plus a **spike counter** row `[1][2][3][4][5]` beneath the trap, with `≥ 2 · SNAP` and `≥ 5 · DIGEST` thresholds annotated.
  - **Fusion idea:** the counting apparatus (trace above, counter below) frames the specimen — the trap is literally wired into the neuroscientist's rig, arguing at a glance that the plant is the counting device.
- **Motion:** one primary spring easing that dramatises the count. Two touches propagate as action-potential pulses along the midrib; each lands as a spike on the trace and increments the counter; on count 2 the trap **snaps shut with a spring**, and the trace then fires the remaining three pulses to light the `DIGEST` indicator. ~6 second loop.
