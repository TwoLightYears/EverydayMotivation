# The galaxy's smallest navigator

**Pairing:** Dung Beetle (*Scarabaeus satyrus*) as Celestial Navigator

## The hook

The nocturnal African dung beetle *Scarabaeus satyrus* is the only known
animal that steers by the Milky Way itself. In planetarium trials with
every bright star hidden and only the galactic band left glowing, the
beetles still rolled their dung balls in dead-straight lines; under
overcast conditions, with the band blotted out, their paths wandered at
random (Dacke, Baird, Byrne, Scholtz & Warrant, *Current Biology* 23,
298–300, 2013).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait).
- **Palette (sampled from the real scene):**
  - `#060912` — night ink (deep sky background, type bed)
  - `#131A2E` — indigo (upper hemisphere, panel fill)
  - `#F0E6D2` — Milky Way cream (galactic haze, body text)
  - `#F4C430` — star gold (brightest stars, accent, section rules)
  - `#C9915A` — Kalahari ochre (dung ball, warm annotation lines)
- **Mood:** quiet, cosmic, meticulous — a star-chart from an observatory
  notebook rather than a pop-sci poster.
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A top-down planetarium disc as the hero: a dark circular arena
    inscribed with compass tick marks, filled with a scatter of stars
    and bisected by a soft diagonal band of Milky Way haze.
  - The beetle in silhouette near the centre of the arena, pushing its
    dung ball along a dashed trajectory that aligns with the galactic
    axis — the composition itself argues the hook.
  - A thin ochre leader to an annotation box reading
    `GALACTIC AZIMUTH ≈ 24°` and the arena diameter `Ø 1 M`.
  - Role tag above the title, Playfair-italic headline below the disc,
    hook paragraph set in Inter at generous measure.
- **Motion:** one primary easing — the beetle's dashed trajectory draws
  itself from the beetle outward along the Milky Way (spring-eased
  stroke reveal), stars fade in on staggered delays, and the compass
  arc marking the beetle's heading sweeps into place. Calm 5-second
  loop; the still frame alone makes the argument.
