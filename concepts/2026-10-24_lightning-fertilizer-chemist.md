# The sky-born chemist

**Pairing:** Lightning Bolt as Fertilizer Chemist

## The hook
A single lightning channel briefly reaches ~30,000 K — roughly five times the Sun's surface temperature — hot enough to shatter the 945 kJ/mol triple bond of atmospheric N₂; the fragments recombine as NO → NO₂ → HNO₃, delivering an estimated 5 Tg of plant-ready nitrate to the Earth's soils every year through rain (Schumann & Huntrieser, *Atmos. Chem. Phys.* 7, 3823, 2007).

## Visual brief
- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 portrait.
- **Palette (lightning's real coloration + its product):**
  - `#060915` deep night (bg ink)
  - `#0F1424` storm sky (frame fill)
  - `#B38CFF` plasma violet (channel edge)
  - `#7FD9FF` electric cyan (channel core)
  - `#F6F2FF` incandescent white (bolt centerline)
  - `#9FE3B0` nitrate green (soil / product accent)
- **Mood:** electric, forensic, meticulous.
- **Key visual elements:**
  - Focal object: one hero lightning bolt as a tall zigzag crossing the frame.
  - Role prop: a chemist's reaction schematic — N≡N split along the channel, recombining into NO · NO₂ · HNO₃ at successive altitude bands.
  - Fusing composition: the bolt itself IS the apparatus. Four horizontal altitude bands annotate each reaction step as the eye travels down the strike; nitrate drops land on a thin soil cross-section at the base with a root receiving NO₃⁻.
- **Motion:** one primary motion — the bolt strikes top-to-ground via a dash-offset reveal eased with a spring; molecules on each altitude band resolve in sequence as the bolt passes (N₂ fractures, NO forms, nitrate drops into soil), then the title and hook spring in. 5 s hold-still-safe hero frame.
