# The Lightning's Sitter

**Pairing:** Fulgurite as Portraitist

## The hook
When a ground-strike lightning bolt (~30,000 K in its core channel — roughly five times the Sun's surface) discharges into silica-rich sand, it melts and fuses the grains along its exact branching path in milliseconds, leaving a hollow glass tube — a *fulgurite*, from Latin *fulgur*, "lightning" — that preserves the strike's three-dimensional geometry as a permanent mineral portrait.

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette** (drawn from real fulgurite + lightning coloration):
  - `#0A0714` — deep night ink (sky / negative space above)
  - `#8FB4E8` — cold electric blue (lightning channel core)
  - `#E6F0FF` — white-hot filament highlight
  - `#F2E7C6` — pale silica sand / cross-section ground
  - `#C6A66A` — weathered amber (fulgurite outer surface)
- **Mood:** reverent, geological, electric
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A single sky-to-ground lightning bolt (branching, jagged, blue-white, ephemeral) in the upper half.
  - The **same** branching geometry, mirrored beneath a horizontal sand line, rendered as a warm amber fulgurite — a geological cross-section: sitter above, likeness below.
  - Draftsman-style stratigraphy tick marks along the sand line; a small mineral-plate label ("SiO₂ · FUSED · 30,000 K").
  - Type block anchored bottom-left: uppercase ROLE tag ("PORTRAITIST"), display italic title, hook paragraph — aligned to the same left margin as the sand-line grid.
- **Motion:** A single-shot strike sequence: the bolt descends (spring easing, ~14 frames), flashes and holds for a beat, then decays as the mirrored fulgurite below the sand line ignites into warm amber — the portrait remains after the sitter is gone. ~5-second loop.
