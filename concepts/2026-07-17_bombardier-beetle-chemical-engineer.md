# The Beetle That Runs a Pulsed Combustion Reactor

**Pairing:** Bombardier Beetle (*Brachinus* spp.) as Chemical Engineer

## The hook
When threatened, the bombardier beetle mixes ~25% hydrogen peroxide and ~10% hydroquinones from two reservoirs into a muscular reaction chamber lined with catalase and peroxidase; the exothermic decomposition flashes the mix to ~100 °C benzoquinone spray and ejects it as a directional pulsed jet at roughly 500 pulses per second — a real, in-vivo pulsed-combustion engine (Eisner & Aneshansley, *PNAS* 96, 1999; Arndt et al., *Science* 348, 2015).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette:**
  - `#0B0D10` — deep charcoal (substrate / negative space)
  - `#12151A` — schematic board (drafting surface)
  - `#E8E0C7` — pale peroxide / paper cream (H₂O₂ reservoir + type)
  - `#F5B841` — hydroquinone amber (reactant + accent type)
  - `#E85A1D` — benzoquinone spray, hot orange-red (jet + heat)
  - `#7A8790` — cool draftsman gray (grid, secondary type, callouts)
- **Mood:** exacting, incendiary, coolly diagrammatic
- **Key visual elements:**
  - Beetle in profile, tail-end pointing toward the upper-right, drawn as a technical silhouette on a blueprint board.
  - Cutaway on the abdomen exposing the two reservoirs, valve, reaction chamber, and rotatable nozzle — the chemical engineer's plant plan.
  - A stroboscopic pulsed jet of hot spray erupting from the nozzle, drawn as a sequence of discrete puffs (dramatizing the 500 Hz pulse).
  - Callouts labelling reactants, catalyst layer, chamber temperature, and pulse rate.
  - Lower-left type block: uppercase ROLE tag, large display title, single-line hook.
- **Motion:** the reservoirs fill (amber + cream), the valve opens, and the nozzle fires a rhythmic strobe of hot pulses toward the top-right — one primary motion that reads at a glance as "pulsed combustion." A 5-second loop with a gentle heat shimmer at the nozzle tip.
