# Coded in Primes

**Pairing:** Periodical Cicada (*Magicicada*) as Cryptographer

## The hook
Periodical cicadas of the genus *Magicicada* spend either 13 or 17 years underground before emerging in a single synchronised brood — and both figures are prime, sharing no common divisor with the 2-, 3-, 4- or 5-year cycles of any periodic predator, so no short-cycle enemy can lock onto them (Cox & Carlton, *American Midland Naturalist* 120, 1988; Yoshimura, *American Naturalist* 149, 1997).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 x 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette:**
  - `#0B0E14` — subterranean ink (background / negative space)
  - `#E7D9B5` — translucent wing-membrane cream (display type)
  - `#D9A03E` — amber wing-vein gold (primes / accents)
  - `#C43420` — Magicicada eye red (the "17" hero glyph)
  - `#5D6152` — muted forest-litter olive (composites, gridlines, small caps)
- **Mood:** cryptic, methodical, quietly resolute
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A colossal italic **17** as the focal glyph, with a stylised Magicicada silhouette anchored inside its counter-form — the composition literally *reads* the pairing.
  - A left-edge rail of 17 stacked year-ticks — a tally counting subterranean years; primes (2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17) glow amber, composites stay muted olive.
  - A secondary smaller `13` echoing the alternate cycle in the upper right.
  - Asymmetric type block low-left: small-caps role tag `MAGICICADA · CRYPTOGRAPHER`, a tight two-word display title, and a single-line hook.
- **Motion:** a spring-eased tally reveals the 17 years one by one along the left rail; the primes flash amber as they arrive; on year 17 an upward glow releases from the ground line, and the giant `17` and cicada silhouette resolve in from below. A single 5-second beat, hero-still safe.
