# The Forge Jupiter Made

**Pairing:** Io (Jupiter's innermost Galilean moon) as Blacksmith

## The hook
Io is the most volcanically active body in the solar system — with more than 400 active volcanoes — but its heat is not radiogenic: the 4:2:1 Laplace resonance with Europa and Ganymede keeps Io's orbit slightly eccentric, so Jupiter's tidal pull continually flexes the moon's solid surface by up to ~100 m, dissipating on the order of 100 TW as heat (predicted by Peale, Cassen & Reynolds in *Science* 203, 1979, days before Voyager 1 photographed the first plumes).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette:** colors read from Voyager / Galileo imagery of Io and the sulfur allotropes on its crust.
  - `#0A0806` — forge black / substrate
  - `#F2D23A` — Io sulfur yellow (dominant surface)
  - `#F08A1F` — magma orange (hot mantle / fresh flows)
  - `#CA3617` — hot ejecta red (vents, caldera cores)
  - `#5A4028` — scorched brown (shadowed terrain, type secondary)
  - `#B9ACA0` — plume ash gray (type primary, measurement rules)
- **Mood:** incandescent, patient, elemental.
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A single hero disc of Io, mottled with real-feeling sulfur blotches and 3 bright hotspots.
  - Jupiter's bulk cropping the right edge of the frame — only a curved limb slice, banded cream/ochre, dwarfing Io.
  - A caliper annotation across Io's belly marking the ~100 m tidal flex; a thin stress-field arc between Jupiter and Io showing the squeeze.
  - A volcanic plume arcing off Io's upper-left limb — the actual Pele/Loki geometry (umbrella-shaped, hundreds of km tall).
  - Lower-left type lockup: role tag, display title, one-line hook.
- **Motion:** one primary motion — a slow spring-eased tidal pulse (≈ 4 s loop). As Io pulses, (a) a stress field between Jupiter and Io brightens, (b) the hotspots glow brighter, (c) the plume rises slightly higher. The caliper number ticks from 0 → 100 m on the first stretch. The forge beat.
