# The Perfumer at the Edge of the Solar System

**Pairing:** Comet 67P/Churyumov–Gerasimenko as Perfumer

## The hook
Between 2014 and 2016, ESA's Rosetta spacecraft flew alongside 67P and sampled its coma with the ROSINA double-focusing mass spectrometer — cataloguing hydrogen sulfide (rotten eggs), ammonia (stables), formaldehyde, hydrogen cyanide (bitter almonds), methanol, sulfur dioxide, and carbon disulfide, plus the amino acid glycine (Altwegg et al., *Science Advances* 2, e1600285, 2016). Team lead Kathrin Altwegg summarised the bouquet as "a heady mixture" — the comet is a 4-km-wide phial of the solar system's original volatiles.

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 x 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette:**
  - `#07080F` — cosmic ink (deep-space substrate)
  - `#2A2620` — comet crust (carbonaceous, albedo ≈ 0.06)
  - `#C9B693` — sunlit dust jet (warm ochre highlight)
  - `#E5B24E` — amber (the perfumer's phial glass)
  - `#7DC0C0` — coma teal (gas-emission fluorescence)
- **Mood:** nocturnal, alchemical, distilled
- **Key visual elements:**
  - The bicone "rubber duck" silhouette of 67P sitting slightly left-of-centre, rendered as a dark crust body with a warm sunlit crescent along its illuminated limb.
  - A single coma plume streaming out to the upper right — soft ochre dust particles fanning into a teal gas envelope — that reads as both cometary tail AND perfumer's atomizer spray.
  - Along the plume: floating molecule cards (H₂S, NH₃, HCN, HCHO, CH₃OH, SO₂) each paired with its scent-note descriptor, arranged like a fragrance-house tasting sheet.
  - Strong lower-left type block: display italic title, uppercase ROLE tag, single-line hook.
  - Faint apothecary/star-chart grid running under the composition, tying the science lab and the sky together.
- **Motion:** the coma plume unfurls outward from the nucleus on a spring easing; molecule cards emerge one after another along the plume in stepped timing; a gentle drift on the outermost gas particles gives a slow 5-second breathing loop.
