# Petrified Lightning

**Pairing:** Lightning as Glassblower

## The hook
When a lightning bolt strikes silica-rich sand, its return-stroke plasma channel briefly reaches around 30,000 K — roughly five times hotter than the Sun's surface, and well above silica's 1,983 K melting point — fusing the grains along its path into a hollow, branched glass tube called a fulgurite (lechatelierite): a bolt petrified in permanent glass.

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette:**
  - `#0A0E1F` — deep storm indigo (sky / negative space)
  - `#FFF2C2` — plasma core (bolt body)
  - `#B6CBFF` — cool bolt halo
  - `#C8964A` — silica sand ochre (substrate)
  - `#E8A661` — molten-glass amber (fulgurite warmth)
- **Mood:** charged, forensic, awestruck
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A single vertical lightning bolt descending from the top of the hero frame and striking a horizon line of sand near the frame's center.
  - Below that horizon, the bolt's "signature" rendered in glass — a branching, root-like fulgurite tube that mirrors the bolt's shape, drawn in warm translucent amber on ochre sand.
  - A specimen-plate frame around the whole scene with corner crop marks, a depth scale, and a thin "SURFACE / SILICA SAND" callout.
  - Left-weighted type lockup below: an uppercase ROLE tag, a two-line italic Playfair title, and a single-line hook paragraph set in Inter.
- **Motion:** a sharp spring-flash of the bolt, then the fulgurite progressively "crystallizes" downward along its branches with staggered easing, followed by a slow afterglow pulse traveling along the trunk. 5-second loop.
