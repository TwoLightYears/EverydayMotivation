# The Pocket-Sized Forge

**Pairing:** Pistol Shrimp (*Alpheus heterochaelis*) as Blacksmith

## The hook
When the pistol shrimp cocks and releases its oversized snapper claw, the water jet it fires reaches ~25 m/s and instantly forms a cavitation bubble whose implosion briefly generates plasma at roughly 5,000 K — comparable to the sun's photosphere — accompanied by a flash of sonoluminescence and a ~218 dB shockwave that can stun small prey. (Versluis, Schmitz, von der Heydt & Lohse, *Science* 289 (2000) 2114–2117; Lohse, Schmitz & Versluis, *Nature* 413 (2001) 477–478.)

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette (drawn from the real coloration of *Alpheus heterochaelis* on a dim seabed, plus the plasma flash):**
  - `#07101C` — abyssal navy (background, cool water)
  - `#122236` — anvil steel-blue (mid ground / grid)
  - `#E85E3D` — shrimp coral (the claw shell)
  - `#F5A24C` — ember amber (claw joints and warm rim light)
  - `#FFEBB0` — plasma highlight / white-hot bubble
  - `#8FA6BF` — cool draftsman gray (secondary type, technical marks)
- **Mood:** taut, incandescent, elemental — a workshop lit by one white spark.
- **Key visual elements:**
  - Profile of the pistol shrimp's cocked snapper claw, drawn as a hinged, hammer-like tool with a pivoting dactyl (the "hammer") and a hollowed propus (the "socket"). Rendered like a smith's plate diagram.
  - The cavitation bubble as the focal event — a small, absurdly bright white-hot orb at the exact strike point where the dactyl slams shut.
  - Concentric shockwave rings expanding from the bubble across a faint seabed grid.
  - A muted technical annotation set: an arrow labelled "≈ 5 000 K" pointing to the bubble; a scale bar; a small anvil-mark corner tag.
  - Strong type block anchored bottom-left: uppercase ROLE tag, large italic display title, one-line hook. Asymmetric layout, generous negative space above the type.
- **Motion:** the dactyl (upper claw finger) pulls back on a spring easing, holds for a taut beat, then snaps closed. On impact, the plasma bubble ignites in a single frame, and a shockwave ring expands outward with cubic ease-out while the bubble decays. 5-second loop.
