# The Wing That Sterilizes Itself

**Pairing:** Cicada Wing (*Psaltoda claripennis*) as Public Health Inspector

## The hook
The transparent membrane of the clanger cicada is covered in a near-regular field of nanoscale pillars — roughly 200 nm tall and 170 nm apart — that mechanically rupture gram-negative bacteria such as *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* on contact: the cell membrane sags between pillars and tears under its own surface tension (Ivanova et al., *Small* 8:2489–2494, 2012). A cicada disinfects its own wing with geometry, not chemistry.
