# The Shrimp That Welds With Sunlight

**Pairing:** Pistol Shrimp (*Alpheus heterochaelis*) as Plasma Welder

## The hook
When the pistol shrimp snaps its oversized claw shut at ~25 m/s, the resulting jet of water forms a cavitation bubble whose violent collapse emits a flash of sonoluminescence measured at roughly **4,700 K** — momentarily within reach of the sun's 5,778 K surface — while delivering a 210 dB shockwave that stuns prey (Lohse, Schmitz & Versluis, *Nature* 413, 2001).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette:**
  - `#070A10` — abyssal deep (background, negative space)
  - `#123140` — cold ocean teal (underwater field, grid)
  - `#E8A86B` — Alpheus shell amber (the shrimp)
  - `#FFE3A3` — cavitation flash highlight (plasma core edge)
  - `#FF5A2C` — hot plasma-arc orange (temperature scale, label accents)
- **Mood:** technical, awed, surgically precise — an engineer's blueprint lit from inside by a small sun
- **Key visual elements:**
  - **Focal object:** a stylized pistol-shrimp claw rendered in profile as a two-part mechanical schematic, mid-snap; the dactyl plunger and socket drawn like a plasma-welder's electrode housing.
  - **Signature prop:** the cavitation bubble at the mouth of the claw — a white-hot plasma core wrapped in concentric shockwave rings, with a drafting-style tick scale reading up through 4,700 K and the sun's 5,778 K marked above it.
  - **Fusion idea:** the claw drawn as "welding equipment" with technical callouts (SPEC: 25 M/S, 210 dB, 4,700 K); the biology and the engineering share the same callouts, one labeled Latin binomial, the other equipment.
- **Motion:** 4-second loop. Charge (claw held open, scale dim) → snap (spring-eased closure, shockwave rings radiate from the plasma point) → flash (plasma core blooms, temperature scale sweeps up to 4,700 K) → settle. A single primary spring motion dramatizes the claw closing, cuing every secondary animation.
