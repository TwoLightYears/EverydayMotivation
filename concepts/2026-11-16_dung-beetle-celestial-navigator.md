# The Beetle Who Navigates by the Galaxy

**Pairing:** Dung Beetle (*Scarabaeus satyrus*) as Celestial Navigator

## The hook
Rolling a freshly-made dung ball away from rivals on a moonless night, the African beetle *Scarabaeus satyrus* keeps its line straight by orienting to the polarisation pattern of the Milky Way itself; in Dacke et al.'s 2013 *Current Biology* study, beetles fitted with tiny cardboard caps that blocked their view of the sky could no longer walk in a straight line — the first documented case of an animal using the galaxy as a compass.

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette (drawn from real coloration):**
  - `#040611` — deep sky night (void beyond the galactic plane)
  - `#0F1A38` — night atmosphere blue
  - `#D9C089` — Milky Way galactic-dust band (warm tone)
  - `#EDEAD6` — star cream (brighter point sources)
  - `#C18B4A` — Scarabaeus elytra bronze (rim light, reticle)
- **Mood:** cosmic, patient, dignified — observatory-quiet.
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A tall celestial chart filling the top ~65% of the frame: the Milky Way arcs diagonally across a dense star field, warm dust band bisecting cooler blues, with a single named target star near the galactic center carrying a crosshair reticle.
  - On the horizon line at the bottom of the chart, a small silhouette of the beetle standing atop its ball, legs splayed, oriented so the ball is between it and the reader — the posture *Scarabaeus* adopts before rolling.
  - A dashed bearing arc rising from the beetle to the reticle: the sightline that argues the pairing.
  - A margin-grid chart rule with tick marks (horizon degrees and declination) frames the composition with observatory restraint.
  - Type lockup below the chart: the ROLE tag ("CELESTIAL NAVIGATOR"), an italic Playfair display title, and the single-line hook, left-aligned to the chart's left margin.
- **Motion:** 5 s loop. (1) Star field and Milky Way fade in. (2) The beetle silhouette materialises on the horizon. (3) The dashed bearing arc *draws* from the beetle upward to the target star along a spring ease, ending in the reticle locking shut. (4) Title springs in and the hook text fades. (5) Stars twinkle at varied phases for the remainder of the loop.
