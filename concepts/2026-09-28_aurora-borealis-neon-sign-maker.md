# The Sky's Neon Sign Maker

**Pairing:** Aurora Borealis as Neon Sign Maker

## The hook
The aurora is a planet-scale neon sign: solar-wind electrons collide with atomic oxygen and nitrogen in the thermosphere, exciting them to quantum states that relax by emitting at discrete line wavelengths — 557.7 nm (green, atomic O), 630.0 nm (red, atomic O), and 427.8 nm (violet, ionised N₂⁺) — the same emission-line mechanism a "neon" (noble-gas) sign uses inside a glass tube, only the tube is 100 km wide and the electrodes are the Sun and the Earth's magnetic field.

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette:**
  - `#050914` — night ink (background / negative space)
  - `#0F1826` — deep atmosphere navy
  - `#5CFF9E` — 557.7 nm atomic-oxygen green (the dominant auroral emission)
  - `#FF5566` — 630.0 nm atomic-oxygen red
  - `#8B7CFF` — 427.8 nm N₂⁺ violet
  - `#8A94A6` — cool draftsman gray (secondary type, rules)
- **Mood:** cold, luminous, precise — a spectrometer with a soul
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A tall undulating aurora **curtain** filling the sky, with the real vertical color stratification: green base, red high-altitude cap, faint violet lower rim.
  - Silhouetted arctic horizon at the bottom of the sky panel.
  - Three thin **wavelength callouts** on the right edge pointing at each color zone — the "sign maker's spec sheet" markup that fuses aurora and neon-sign vocabulary.
  - A visible-spectrum ruler beneath the sky with ticks at 428, 558, 630 nm — the emission-line schematic.
  - Type lockup lower-left: uppercase role tag → display italic title → single-line hook.
- **Motion:** the curtain sways along a soft sine wave (primary motion, spring-eased), while the three emission callouts fade in in order — violet, green, red — and the spectrum ticks pop into place. 5-second loop.
