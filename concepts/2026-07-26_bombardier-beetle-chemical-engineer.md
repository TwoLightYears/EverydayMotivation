# The Pulsejet Chemist

**Pairing:** Bombardier Beetle (*Brachinus* spp.) as Chemical Engineer

## The hook
The bombardier beetle's abdomen houses a two-chamber reactor: a reservoir of hydroquinones and hydrogen peroxide feeds through a one-way valve into a thick-walled combustion chamber lined with catalase and peroxidase, where the enzyme-catalyzed reaction ejects a boiling ~100 °C benzoquinone spray in a pulse-jet stream measured at roughly 500 pulses per second — the biological pulsejet mechanism described by Dean, Aneshansley, Edgerton, and Eisner in *Science* 248 (1990): 1219–1221.
