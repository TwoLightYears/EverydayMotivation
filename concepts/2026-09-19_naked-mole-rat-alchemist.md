# The Alchemist Beneath the Ground

**Pairing:** Naked Mole-Rat (*Heterocephalus glaber*) as Alchemist

## The hook
When its burrow drops to near-zero oxygen, the naked mole-rat transmutes its own fuel: brain and heart switch from glucose to **fructose**, driving anaerobic ATP synthesis through **fructokinase (KHK)** and the **GLUT5** transporter — a metabolic pathway plants use freely, but no other mammal — letting it survive up to **18 minutes of total anoxia** without cell death (Park et al., *Science* 356 (2017): 307–311).

## Visual brief

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait, social poster)
- **Palette:**
  - `#171009` — deep earth ink (substrate / burrow negative space)
  - `#F0B39D` — mole-rat skin flesh (focal warm)
  - `#F5D89C` — incisor ivory / highlight
  - `#B87333` — alchemist copper (transmutation accent, glyphs, arrows)
  - `#7C6250` — warm draftsman gray (grid, secondary type)
- **Mood:** warm, cryptic, exacting — an apothecary's plate crossed with a lab notebook.
- **Key visual elements:**
  - A stylised anatomical study of the mole-rat in profile, drawn as a copper-line engraving, with the **heart** and **brain** marked by small copper glyphs (☿ mercury for brain, ♀ copper for heart) — the two organs known to switch to fructose.
  - An alchemical transmutation glyph on the opposite side of the frame: two hexagon sugar rings labelled **GLUCOSE** and **FRUCTOSE**, an alchemical arrow between them carrying the labels **KHK · GLUT5**, and a small annotation "18 MIN · 0% O₂".
  - Corner apparatus: an old-style scale bar / cartouche band that mimics a Renaissance alchemist's plate margin, with a fine grid underlaid across the whole plate.
  - Strong type block below the plate: uppercase ROLE tag ("ALCHEMIST"), a large italic display title, and a single-line hook that names KHK and 18 minutes.
- **Motion:** on the transmutation glyph, the **GLUCOSE** hexagon dissolves into copper particles and reassembles as the **FRUCTOSE** hexagon along a spring curve; simultaneously, the heart glyph and the brain glyph light up copper. Meanwhile, an "O₂ 21% → 0%" ticker drops. A single, calm 5-second beat, then a slow copper pulse along the arrow to loop.
